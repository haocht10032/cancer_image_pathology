## Original software: MIT

Original project software is licensed under the [MIT license](LICENSE).
This includes original portions of `Methods/`, `scripts/`, Python and R analysis
scripts, executable demo JavaScript/CSS/HTML, configurations, and code cells/code
chunks in notebooks and Rmd documents.

Third-party and adapted portions are not automatically covered. In particular,
`Methods/BaselineCNN/maskcut_preprocess.py` is **excluded from this MIT grant**
pending the provenance review in [THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md](THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md).
This exclusion is not a determination of that file's upstream license.
File-specific upstream notices and terms take precedence for upstream material.

The contributor copyright notice identifies contributors to the original
software; manuscript authorship alone does not establish ownership of every file.

## Exclusions and upstream rights

weights, checkpoints, provider-restricted features, or copied/adapted upstream
software. These materials remain subject to their applicable terms.
Imports and calls to separately installed dependencies do not relicense them.

Known upstream sources, file-specific exceptions, and remaining review items are
listed in [THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md](THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md). Preserve required
upstream notices and terms for any copied/adapted components.
